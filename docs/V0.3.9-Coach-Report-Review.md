## Report options

Match Analysis now offers **Coach Report** (recommended/default report choice) and **Full Match Data**. Read-only Match History offers both. The existing Full-Time “VIEW DETAILED MATCH REPORT” action continues to open the detailed data output. Report choice is UI state only; no match data is persisted by selecting, viewing or printing either output.

Coach Report uses five portrait-A4 sections:

1. Match summary: compact populated metadata, Gaelic score with smaller total in parentheses, H1/H2/recorded FT score, five comparison metrics and 3–5 evidence-based observations where eligible.
2. Shooting: the existing corrected shared pitch (our attacking goal TOP), outcome legend, plotted/unlocated counts, shared Inside/Outside 40m conversion, source scores/attempts and H1/H2 comparison.
3. Kickouts & goalkeepers: existing goalkeeper calculations and time, two compact cards, kicking-team retention comparison, lengths, Marks, OOP and explicit credited possession winners. Existing team/GK denominator distinctions are disclosed; no calculator is changed.
4. Meaningful player contributions only, grouped by team. Eight readable columns replace the wide matrix. Scores, shot attempts/blocked attempts, KO possession wins, Marks, TO won/lost, interceptions and defensive blocks remain distinct. GK and other attributed event-only participants qualify; unused substitutes are omitted. Unattributed events remain in match totals, never allocated to players. Extra contributors can continue rather than shrinking text.
5. Both-team half comparison, compact mathematically supported scoring runs and substitutions. No complete scoring/event timeline is dumped into Coach Report.

The complete team comparison, unused squad rows, wide player matrix, detailed supporting data, scoring timeline, notes, goalkeeper and kickout detail remain in **Full Match Data**. No underlying analysis has been removed. Its title/routes changed; the body remains the existing detailed output including the prior uncommitted 40m corrections.

## New observation rules

All read existing aggregates/events; no generative service, tactical attribution, rating, assist or possession object exists.

- H1/H2 conversion comparison: at least five attempts in each half.
- Our kicking-team retention: at least five known outcomes.
- Long kickout retention: at least five known Long outcomes.
- Opposition retention and our wins: at least five known outcomes; OOP is disclosed and never given a player credit.
- Outside-40m conversion: at least three classifiable attempts.
- A scorer's share: at least three successful attempts and at least half of our recorded scores.
- At most five observations in the above priority order. Small/no-data matches display a neutral insufficient-evidence statement.
- Match flow: consecutive scoring events for one team within one half, at least three scores / four points, finite times with distinct endpoints. Only the highest-points qualifying run per team is shown, with exact half, times and Gaelic score. Runs do not cross an opponent's score or half-time. They describe recorded scoring only, not momentum or a tactical cause.

## Actual supplied PDF reviews: provenance and limitations

The two ten-page PDFs were used as real factual sources. Exact originals and PDF-snapshot display models are retained under `tests/fixtures/coach-report/`. The models include source SHA-256 hashes. They are NOT reconstructed saved matches or new event logs.

- **SML v Naomh Ciaran:** 2-10 (16) vs 5-17 (32); 121 recorded events; 27 meaningful player/GK rows. H1/H2 totals and the complete 34-score sequence reconcile. Observations include conversion 38.5%→43.8%, retention 38.7%, Long retention 26.7%, opposition retention 58.3%, and #14's six of twelve scores. The largest qualifying opposition run in the printed sequence is 2-4 without reply in H2, 17:56–26:42; no example wording has been assumed as fact.
- **Rynaghs v Shamrocks:** 2-15 (21) vs 1-11 (14); 114 recorded events; 27 meaningful player/GK rows. H1/H2 totals and the complete 29-score sequence reconcile. Observations include conversion 44.4%→52.9%, retention 58.3%, Long retention 44.4%, opposition retention 55.6%. Printed sequence supports Rynaghs 0-5 without reply H1 00:20–09:03 and Shamrocks 0-6 H1 09:52–20:30.

Player cells were read by actual PDF column/row geometry. The old narrow conversion column wrapped digits across pages; player conversion was derived from the printed scoring/attempt counts using the existing formula, not guessed from broken glyph order. Player totals can be below team totals because unattributed events were not distributed to people. Names and jerseys are retained exactly as printed, including old source text such as truncated names or section text; these are not new importer results or new player identities.

These PDFs do not contain original per-event shot coordinates, source-attempt denominators, per-half kickout outcomes or substitution event details. The review samples explicitly show those fields as unavailable. They do NOT invent corrected location statistics or remove/move the observed far-end shots. The actual application report reads saved events and supplies these fields normally; no additional match-day input is required. A separate fictional saved-match example demonstrates the complete corrected pitch/40m report.
