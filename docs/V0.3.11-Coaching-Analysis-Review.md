# V0.3.11 development review

Base: ec4678f095b6f9493cf8ee99544b0df194e1b3d8. Local development only.

## Shared evidence and presentation

`coachingInsights` interprets the existing canonical, validity-filtered event
stream. Its one selection is shared by Full-Time/Normal-Time Analysis and the
existing printable Coach Report. Summary findings occupy Key Observations;
chronological findings occupy Match Flow. They are disjoint subsets of the same
five findings (three at Half Time), rather than two independent allowances.
Existing statistics, shot maps, goalkeeper sections, match history and workflows
remain. The Coach Report still has five pages; it uses shorter wording of the
same selected findings. Source-PDF display snapshots retain their supplied
observations because they are not full event ledgers.

## Materiality

- Attack comparisons: five attempts per half; volume difference at least three
  attempts and 25%; conversion gap at least 15 percentage points. Within ten
  points counts as similar conversion; similar volume must be within 25%.
  Output is scoreboard points, conversion is successful attempts / attempts.
- Located shots: three per location sample and a 20-point conversion gap.
  Unknown/invalid locations stay outside these comparisons.
- Kickout contrasts: five known outcomes in each compared sample and a 25-point
  difference. Unknown outcomes excluded; OOP is one loss for the kicking team.
- Player defensive findings: three attributed actions in a single category and
  at least half of that team's attributed category. One isolated action cannot
  produce a performance observation. Marks are a subset of kickout wins, and
  event IDs are sets, never summed as independent contributions.
- Scoring influence: three successful attempts and at least half the team's
  recorded scoreboard points. A two-pointer is one attempt, worth two points.
- Goalkeeper restart contribution: eight known outcomes, at least 75% retained;
  no receiver is inferred. Save recognition requires an explicit Saved miss
  and existing goalkeeper attribution; a Short miss alone is not called a save.
- Defensive half changes: at least six actions across H1/H2, difference of three.

All are descriptive, recorded-evidence thresholds, not statistical certainty or
player ratings. Optional Pass volume is never influence evidence.

## Selection

Stage 1 Match Flow rules, significance gates, evidence deduplication and its
no-arbitrary-time-limit policy are unchanged. All eligible merged Flow stories
participate in shared selection, not just the first five. Their priorities are
scaled to 13–30 so scoring impact is comparable with material aggregate
contrasts. After each selection, another story in the same dimension receives
a six-point redundancy penalty. This is about breadth of evidence, not quotas:
no polarity or dimension is guaranteed a slot. Raw significance is retained;
selectionScore explains the ordering. Semantic duplicate topics for the same
team/player are selected once. Identical evidence for period/length contrasts
is one finding with supporting context.

Momentum continuation describes the next three recorded scores only when at
least two scores and a net three-point movement support it. Unknown/invalid
ordering stops that interpretation. It adds context, not another ranking bonus
or a causal claim. Existing within-half scoring-run extracts in the detailed
report remain statistical reference detail, not another observation selector.

## Boundaries

No event-age or nominal-period cutoff. Additional time stays in its recorded
period; local sequences stop at evidence/period boundaries. Cross-period score
swings remain. ET is included in whole-match figures but never folded into H1/H2
comparisons. Shootout outcomes remain separate. Unknown timing does not erase
scores or invent a timeline. No storage/schema migration or match edits.

## Verification limitations

Review fixtures are synthetic. The real SML/Naomh Ciaran and Rynaghs/Shamrocks
PDF snapshots contain aggregates, not complete ledgers. Manchans/Vincents has
partial factual score checkpoints only. No missing real events are recreated.
Physical iPad acceptance is still required before release/deployment.

Nine portable golden-statistic tests use the existing synthetic fixtures against
values captured from the exact V0.3.10 commit. They compare both teams, all four
periods, full-match totals, kickouts, defensive figures and player scoring. The
golden JSON contains calculated synthetic statistics, not an exported real match.
